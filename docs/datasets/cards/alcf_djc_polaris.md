DIM_JOB_COMPOSITE job accounting from Argonne's Polaris supercomputer, 2022-08 through 2026-07 in five files, from the ALCF public data catalog: Cobalt/PBS scheduler records with requested walltime (WALLTIME_SECONDS) and elapsed runtime (RUNTIME_SECONDS), cores and nodes used and requested, queue, exit status, machine, award category and science field. Users and projects are anonymized at source (USERNAME_GENID, PROJECT_NAME_GENID). ALCF gates downloads behind a one-time name and email form, so fetch the files yourself; `datasets prepare --from <dir>` then checks and normalizes them (see docs/datasets/alcf-djc-ingest.md).
