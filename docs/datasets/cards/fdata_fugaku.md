# F-DATA (Fugaku Jobs, 2023–2024)

`dataset.fdata_fugaku` · version 2.0.0 · system Fugaku · serves job runtime and job power

> Generated by `hpc-oda datasets cards` from the dataset's descriptor, its runtime card, the error ceilings, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_dataset_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Job records of Fugaku (Fujitsu A64FX) from F-DATA, by RIKEN R-CCS and the University of Bologna. The source spans March 2021–April 2024 in 38 monthly files (about 24M jobs, 28 GB), each holding the jobs submitted in one month, Japan time. This curation takes March–September 2023 and December 2023–April 2024, so October and November 2023 are absent. Runtime is duration (seconds). Power is mapped whole-job (avgpcon, maxpcon, in watts) and per requested node (avgpcon_per_node, maxpcon_per_node), and each row carries F-DATA's precomputed job embedding. Fugaku takes periodic maintenance outages of about three days.
