# F-DATA (Fugaku Jobs, Mar–Sep 2023)

`dataset.fdata_fugaku` · version 1.3.0 · system Fugaku · serves job runtime and job power

> Generated by `hpc-oda datasets cards` from the dataset's descriptor, its runtime card, the error ceilings, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_dataset_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Job records of Fugaku (Fujitsu A64FX) from F-DATA, by RIKEN R-CCS and the University of Bologna. The source spans March 2021–April 2024 in 38 monthly parquet files (about 24M jobs, 28 GB); this curation takes March–September 2023. Runtime is duration (seconds). Average and peak job power are mapped whole-job (avgpcon, maxpcon) and per requested node (avgpcon_per_node, maxpcon_per_node). Fugaku takes periodic maintenance outages of about three days.
