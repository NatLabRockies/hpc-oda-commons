# F-DATA (Fugaku Jobs, Dec 2023–Apr 2024, UoPC window)

`dataset.fdata_fugaku_uopc` · version 1.0.0 · system Fugaku · serves job runtime and job power

> Generated by `hpc-oda datasets cards` from the dataset's descriptor, its runtime card, the error ceilings, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_dataset_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Job records of Fugaku (Fujitsu A64FX) from F-DATA, by RIKEN R-CCS and the University of Bologna: December 2023–April 2024, the months the UoPC paper evaluates on. Power is mapped whole-job (avgpcon, maxpcon, in watts) and per requested node (avgpcon_per_node, maxpcon_per_node). Each row also carries F-DATA's precomputed job embedding.
