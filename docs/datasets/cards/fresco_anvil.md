# FRESCO Anvil Job Accounting

`dataset.fresco_anvil` · version 1.0.0 · system Anvil · serves job runtime

> Generated by `hpc-oda datasets cards` from the dataset's descriptor, its runtime card, the error ceilings, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_dataset_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Anonymized Slurm job accounting from Purdue's Anvil supercomputer (NVIDIA A100), from the FRESCO repository: 11 monthly CSVs, July 2022–May 2023. Runtime is Wall Time and requested walltime is Requested Wall Time (seconds). Timestamps are ISO with a trailing "UTC" word. Cores, GPUs and nodes, requested nodes, queue, exit status and wait time are mapped; users and accounts are anonymized at source.
