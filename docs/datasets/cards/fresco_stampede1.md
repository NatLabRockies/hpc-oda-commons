# FRESCO Stampede1 Job Accounting

`dataset.fresco_stampede1` · version 1.0.0 · system Stampede · serves job runtime

> Generated by `hpc-oda datasets cards` from the dataset's descriptor, its runtime card, the error ceilings, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_dataset_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Slurm job accounting of TACC's Stampede supercomputer (Stampede1), from the FRESCO repository: one CSV of about 8.7M jobs, 2013–2018. Runtime is end_time − start_time, and requested walltime is the walltime column (minutes); timestamps are MM/DD/YYYY. Users, accounts and job ids are anonymized at source. Rows whose end precedes their start are dropped, and allocated_cpus must be at least 1; a couple of rows with corrupt epoch-era start times remain.
