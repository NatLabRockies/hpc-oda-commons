# NLR Eagle Jobs (anonymized)

`dataset.nlr_eagle` · version 1.1.0 · system Eagle · serves job runtime and job power

> Generated by `hpc-oda datasets cards` from the dataset's descriptor, its runtime card, the error ceilings, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_dataset_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Anonymized Slurm sacct job records of NLR's Eagle system over its 2019–2024 lifetime (about 13.8M jobs), as a zipped Hive-partitioned Parquet dataset: the job records, not the separate energy variant. Runtime is end_time − start_time from the DST-correct timestamp columns; requested walltime is wallclock_req. Users and accounts are hashed at source. Average power: node_energy_total_watt_hours is the job's energy summed over its nodes, so whole-job watts are energy ÷ runtime (avgpcon) and per node that ÷ nodes_used (avgpcon_per_node), which matches the energy variant's per-node averages exactly. A 0 Wh reading means no reading and becomes null, as do per-node values outside the 0.1st–99.9th percentiles, since a few readings are impossible. There is no peak power.
