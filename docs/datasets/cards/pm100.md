# PM100 (Marconi100 Jobs)

`dataset.pm100` · version 1.1.0 · system Marconi100 · serves job runtime and job power

> Generated by `hpc-oda datasets cards` from the dataset's descriptor, its runtime card, the error ceilings, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_dataset_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Per-job Slurm accounting of Marconi100 (CINECA), curated as PM100 from the M100 ExaData job_table: 231,238 jobs, May–October 2020, with runtime, requested time limit, requested and allocated nodes, cores and GPUs, and per-job power. node_power_consumption is the job's total node power in watts across its allocated nodes, sampled every 20 s (about 700 W per node). Its mean and maximum give avgpcon and maxpcon, and per allocated node avgpcon_per_node and maxpcon_per_node.
