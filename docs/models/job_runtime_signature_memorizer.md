# Signature memorizer

`model.job_runtime_signature_memorizer` · version 0.1.0 · predicts job runtime and job power

> Generated by `hpc-oda models cards` from the code, the registry, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_model_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

Trivial-but-strong baseline: predicts the median target of jobs whose submit-time features are identical, drawn from the rolling training window. No fitting. Backs off by dropping features cheapest-first when nothing matches, and reports exact-match coverage alongside its metrics.
