# MLP

`model.mlp` · version 0.1.0 · predicts job runtime

> Generated by `hpc-oda models cards` from the code, the registry, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_model_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

A feed-forward neural network (scikit-learn MLPRegressor) over the job's submit-time features, with categorical columns one-hot encoded and reduced by truncated SVD.

Not on the [leaderboard](https://natlabrockies.github.io/hpc-oda-commons/leaderboard/) of 2026-10-21.
