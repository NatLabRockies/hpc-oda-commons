# UoPC

`model.uopc` · version 0.1.0 · predicts job power

> Generated by `hpc-oda models cards` from the code, the registry, the bundled recipes and the published leaderboard. Don't edit it by hand: `tests/unit/test_model_cards.py` fails when it differs from what the generator writes.

User-based online power prediction (UoPC, Antici et al., ISC 2025): a per-user kNN over each user's newest finished jobs, predicting the median of the k nearest. It runs on the shared rolling windows (the power benchmark), on the paper's date-cutoff protocol, and on a fixed split; the paper-comparison recipes keep the authors' vote over whole watts.
